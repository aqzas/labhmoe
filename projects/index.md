# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-wrench" %}Projects

Mesenchymal stromal cells (MSCs) can be obtained from sources including bone marrow, adipose tissue, umbilical cord and placenta. Their differentiation potential and immunomodulatory properties make them promising candidates for tissue repair. By secreting cytokines and growth factors, MSCs can regulate immune responses and help restore tissue homeostasis.

Extracellular vesicles (EVs) carry lipids, proteins and RNA between cells and participate in intercellular communication, immune regulation and other biological processes. Exosomes are a subtype of EVs. MSCs and their EVs are being investigated for tissue repair, disease diagnosis and therapeutic delivery.
